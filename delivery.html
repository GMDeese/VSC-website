The Framework

Five stages. Zero gaps.

Each stage has defined inputs, outputs, and acceptance criteria. Engagements don't move forward without evidence.

01
Discovery

Segment & Discover

We identify the right customer segments through structured research — interviews, observational study, and quantitative data — then synthesize insights into prioritized hypotheses. This isn't a survey. It's a rigorous investigation.

Stage Deliverables

  • Customer segment map with validation evidence
  • User research synthesis (verbatims, patterns, jobs-to-be-done)
  • Prioritized insight stack
  • Hypothesis backlog for next stage
02
Definition

Define Value

Evidence-based value proposition construction. We turn insights into assumptions, and assumptions into testable hypotheses. Every claim is tied to user evidence — not executive opinion. This stage is where most products win or lose before they're built.

Stage Deliverables

  • Value proposition canvas (validated)
  • Assumption map (ranked by risk)
  • Competitive positioning framework
  • Riskiest Assumption Test (RAT) designs
03
Design & Validate

Prototype & Test

We design against the riskiest assumptions first — then run structured usability tests before any production code is committed. This stage eliminates the most expensive mistake in product development: building the wrong thing.

Stage Deliverables

  • Lo-fi and hi-fi prototype artifacts
  • Usability test protocol and execution
  • Test findings synthesis
  • Design iteration log
  • Go/no-go recommendation with evidence
04
Build

Build & Measure

Product leadership through the build phase — evidence-based backlog prioritization, resource management, design review, and real-time performance measurement. We set the instrumentation strategy before engineering writes code.

Stage Deliverables

  • Prioritized product backlog (with evidence scores)
  • Sprint planning and capacity framework
  • Analytics instrumentation plan
  • Product-market fit measurement protocol
05
Go-to-Market

Ship & Scale

Launch strategy, positioning, and GTM execution. We don't hand you a playbook and leave — we activate the GTM Engine. The launch becomes a managed, instrumented campaign from day one, not a hope-and-post-on-LinkedIn.

Stage Deliverables

  • GTM strategy and channel plan
  • Launch sequencing playbook
  • PMF testing framework (Ellis benchmark)
  • Scale decision criteria
  • GTM Engine activation — see below

Stage 5 Capability

The GTM Engine.

Built internally at VSC and battle-tested on Stillwater's own launch. When we hit the Ship & Scale phase, we don't hand you a deck — we activate a full automated go-to-market pipeline. Four components, running in parallel, from day one.

Ad Generator

AI-powered creative generation for paid channels. Produces copy, headlines, and structured ad variants at scale — tuned to the positioning defined in Stage 2. No agency markup, no creative bottleneck.

Landing Pages

Conversion-optimized landing pages generated from the validated value proposition. Structured for A/B testing across segments identified in Stage 1. Built to measure, not just to ship.

Outbound Pipeline

Automated outreach sequencing to the target segments mapped in discovery. Personalized at the segment level, timed across channels, and tracked against reply and conversion benchmarks.

Dashboard & Attribution

Real-time analytics across every GTM channel. Attribution by source, segment, and campaign. The data feeds back into the PMF testing protocol and informs the scale decision — close the loop from launch day one.

Status: The GTM Engine is currently deployed internally for Stillwater and being generalized for external E-2-E engagements. Inquire during your strategy session if this capability is relevant to your launch timeline.
